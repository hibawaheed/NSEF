READING THE SIGNAL

A change is a reason
to look closer.

SubPSI compares distributions inside predefined groups. It is an investigation tool, not a verdict about fairness.

01

Choose what to compare

Provide separate reference and current CSVs. Choose one feature and up to two grouping attributes. A group might be an age band; an intersection combines two attributes.

02

Measure the difference

For numeric data, bins are designed using a reserved 25% of reference rows. The remaining reference rows and current data are counted in those same bins. PSI measures how different these proportions are.

Categorical data includes separate bins for unseen categories and missing values.

03

Check the noise

Permutation tests ask how often random reassignment of dataset labels could produce a difference at least this large. Holm correction accounts for every selected group and batch together at a 5% family error level, under independent-sample assumptions.

04

Keep the uncertainty

Insufficient records produce an explicit “insufficient” result. No corrected alert means the test did not find enough evidence; it does not establish that the distributions are identical.

What this version can—and cannot—establish

  • Input drift does not establish prediction failure or unfairness. Labelled performance audits in the research study are separate from this input-only browser analyzer.
  • Small groups need more data. Accumulation waits for 100 records and resets after each assessment; this can delay detection and mix observations from before and after a change.
  • Rows should represent independent observations. Arbitrarily correlated time-series readings, repeated individuals and value-based sampling do not inherit the permutation guarantee.
  • The error correction covers one analysis. Trying many new features or uploading new batches repeatedly creates additional tests beyond that guarantee.
  • The browser analyzer uses a held-out reference design split and one Holm family across all selected batches. The research uses separate design data and an online allocation of alpha across 20 batches. Their reported numbers are not interchangeable.
  • Large PSI values are not probabilities or effect sizes for harm. The significance threshold is applied to adjusted p-values, not directly to PSI.

Adult: Becker, B. & Kohavi, R. (1996), UCI Machine Learning Repository, CC BY 4.0. DOI: 10.24432/C5XW20. The published study used deduplication, separate data roles and controlled resampling.
