## Consequence for this revision

No new algorithmic claim is currently supported. Strongest feasible candidate: a transparent matched-statistic study that shows when subgroup support and fixed-reference scarcity dominate the apparent benefit of accumulation, with real-year/cross-cohort observations separated from constructed interventions. This is a hypothesis for an empirical study, not a promised positive result. If PSI and KS tie, or KS is preferable, retain that conclusion. Bounded-age collection is not a new idea just because attached to PSI. Omit its main experimental claim under the present short natural timelines.
