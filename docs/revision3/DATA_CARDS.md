# Revision 3 source and feasibility cards

AI-assisted source audit, 11 October 2026. All results here are source/schema/support checks. Main natural-data drift and prediction evaluation has not run. Exact access times, source URLs, hashes and file sizes are machine-recorded in the linked local manifests.

## ACS Massachusetts person records

- Collector: United States Census Bureau, American Community Survey. Cite: U.S. Census Bureau, *ACS 1-Year Public Use Microdata Sample, Massachusetts person records*, each of 2015–2019; `csv_pma.zip`; accessed 11 October 2026. [Official access](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/microdata/access.html), [year-specific documentation](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/microdata/documentation.html).
- Access/reuse: directly available without credentials or fees. Use for statistical research with Census attribution and no implication of endorsement. [Census citation/public-use statement](https://www.census.gov/about/policies/citation.html); [Census use terms referenced by Folktables](https://www.census.gov/data/developers/about/terms-of-service.html). No identification of respondents or identity-producing linkage. These public-use terms are distinct from Folktables' **MIT software licence**. No claim that the data are CC0 or that an MIT software licence licenses all data.
- Release: five distinct 1-Year archives, not five overlapping 5-Year files. Original ZIP bytes remain ignored/local at `data/raw/revision3/acs/<year>/csv_pma.zip`; HTTP Last-Modified/ETag (when supplied), ZIP CRC inventory and SHA-256 receipts accompany them. No publisher-supplied SHA-256 was located; local hashes record exactly what was retrieved, rather than externally certifying it.
- Counts and selected-field checks: `results/raw/revision3/acs-feasibility/acs_schema.json`; all subgroup/role/label-denominator counts: `acs_support.csv`. Raw rows total 348,256. Proposed eligible ages 16–90: 57,131 / 57,403 / 57,806 / 58,502 / 59,139. No duplicate SERIALNO+SPORDER keys within year; all ST=25. Selected age, schooling, sex, race and adult ESR fields have no missing values. Schema output retains observed codes and allocation counts, so no-missing is not confused with no imputation.
- Time: survey release year. No interview-day/month timeline is inferred from ordering, QTRBIR (birth quarter), or record identifiers. Person serial formats changed; retain strings and namespace by year. Key uniqueness does not prove different years contain independent people.
- Demographics: published SEX=1/2 and RAC1P=1…9. Full category definitions remain in each source dictionary; racial categories are not converted to a binary attribute. Recorded sex is not inferred gender identity. All 30 views are retained, even empty groups.
- Labels: proposed ESR==1, explicitly “civilian employed, at work.” ESR2 is temporarily absent employed; ESR3 unemployed; ESR4/5 armed forces; ESR6 outside labour force. The target is not general employment. Proposed ages16–90 follow an explicit benchmark adaptation. The current Folktables source applies no age filter although its paper/README describes one; this discrepancy is documented, not silently inherited.
- Inputs: AGEP numeric and SCHL educational order, codes1–24. Year-specific 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018 and 2019 dictionaries were inspected for AGEP/SCHL/SEX/RAC1P/ESR. Endpoints have consistent selected code meanings. 2019 changes RELP to RELSHIPP and other unused fields; no blind concatenation of all features. Dictionary download failures for initially guessed 2015/2016 paths were resolved using official `PUMSDataDict15.txt`/`PUMSDataDict16.txt` links.
- Dependence/quality: [2019 Accuracy of PUMS](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/tech_docs/pums/accuracy/2019AccuracyPUMS.pdf) describes household selection, weights and disclosure protections. Household members share sampling and covariates; published records may have imputation, perturbation, swapping, top/bottom coding or collapsed detail. PWGTP and all 80 replicate weights exist in each file (case of weight headers differs in2015/2016). Main estimand is selected-record, unweighted and descriptive; these are not calibrated survey-population tests.
- Roles and lineage: whole households assigned before models/detectors; private CSVs in `data/raw/revision3/lineage/`, with file hashes in schema JSON. No raw keys in public figure CSVs. 2015 is fit/design/calibration,2016 reference/audit,2017–2019 monitor/audit. Census IDs cannot be used to certify cross-year independence.
- Limits: one US state, three held-out annual comparisons, simplified benchmark label/model. Twelve of30 reference views cannot meet100 (or50) observations. No minority guarantee and no natural detection-accuracy ground truth.

## OULAD compact cohort case

- Collector: Open University administrative/learning systems; dataset creators Jakub Kuzilek, Martin Hlosta and Zdenek Zdrahal. Cite [UCI dataset, DOI10.24432/C5KK69](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/349/open+university+learning+analytics+dataset), donated2015; [data paper, DOI10.1038/sdata.2017.171](https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata2017171),2017. UCI explicitly licenses the dataset **CC BY 4.0**, allowing reuse with attribution.
- Exact source: original UCI ZIP URL, accessed11 October 2026. We saved its first1,546,601bytes, enough to extract `courses.csv`, `studentInfo.csv`, and `studentRegistration.csv`. Each selected member's size and ZIP-header CRC were verified, then member/prefix SHA-256 recorded. This is a compact retrieval of actual original bytes, **not a fabricated replacement ZIP and not a verified full archive**. `extract_oulad_metadata.py` can repeat the retrieval without downloading VLE clicks. Local receipt: `data/raw/revision3/oulad/metadata/receipt.json`; public provenance copy in feasibility schema JSON. The larger interrupted `.part` download is not analysis input.
- Schema: enrolment key(module,presentation,student) unique in both student tables; one-to-one join checked. Full studentInfo has32,593rows,28,785distinct student IDs. Raw source uses `?` for missing dates; explicit parser handles it. The initial parser failure is preserved in `records/revision3/oulad_feasibility_log.txt`, and its incomplete output directory is marked unsuccessful.
- Selection: alphabetically first module with>=500rows in both2013J and2014J is BBB. Inventory includes every module/presentation, not only the chosen pair. J is October; B and J are not mixed. Restrict to registered and active by day0, exclude unknown registration dates and withdrawal at/before day0. Never feed future withdrawal time to the model. Remove later-cohort students present in the earlier cohort, without using outcomes. All selection/exclusion counts are recorded.
- Actual results: selected pair4,529rows;650day0-ineligible;44eligible later repeat enrolments removed;3,835retained. Distinct student overlap before filtering47. 2013J1,883 and2014J1,952. Final result codes verified as Pass/Distinction/Fail/Withdrawn; selected predictors/grouping fields/labels have no missing values.
- Proposed predictors: previous attempts and studied credits at module entry; label successful completion(Pass/Distinction) vs Fail/Withdrawn. No VLE/assessment values used. Every enrolment contributes one feature vector, not repeated clicks. Groups are recorded gender and disability with all four intersections. Local private student-role lineage is hashed, not exported.
- Limits: two selected historical cohorts with different course lengths268/262days; selection/anonymisation affect representativeness. Repeated learners exist in the full source. One later cohort cannot establish long-stream monitoring or onset. Only4/9 proposed reference views meet50/100 records; positive/negative label support is separate. Withdrawn is not a synonym for academic failure. Natural outputs remain descriptive.

## Inspection boundary

Group counts and positive/negative label denominators were inspected to establish support. No later-year feature distribution statistics, drift scores, fitted predictions or outcome gaps were evaluated. These are not completely unseen files, and the main plan says exactly what remains held out. No patient/private/recruited data, no identity linkage, and no raw identifier export were introduced.
