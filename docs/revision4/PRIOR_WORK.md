Repository comments and generic web discussions were not used as primary evidence. Search hits on older KS efficiency papers were not inspected enough to claim a methods review. No restricted article text has been copied into this repository.

The defensible candidate is an **empirical investigation**: historical sensitivity under a realistic multiplicity budget, followed by a transparent count-target comparison using identical records, explicit shortages and preserved negative results. The statistical components are established. We have not established an original algorithm or a general power guarantee. Evidence needed is the approved sensitivity study, held-out calibration validation, null checks, matched policy outcomes and complete costs. If it fails, the corrected evidence and limits remain useful research findings.
